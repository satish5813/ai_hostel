KL University Hostels · demo for the President

Demo runbook

One student, one phone, every operation on the live system. About 20 minutes. The staff steps happen by themselves a few seconds after the student acts.

Before the demo (10 minutes)

  1. Start the API if it is not running (it answers at http://localhost:4100/api/auth/demo-accounts).
  2. Put the demo back to its starting point: 
    /Users/aasrithatechnologies/Desktop/hostel_automation/scripts/reset-demo.sh
  3. Start the assistant that plays the wardens, the office, the mess and maintenance, and leave it running in its own Terminal window. Its lines are good to show on a second screen: 
    /Users/aasrithatechnologies/Desktop/hostel_automation/scripts/run-demo-assistant.sh
  4. Start the phones: 
    /Users/aasrithatechnologies/Desktop/hostel_automation/scripts/run-mobile-demo.sh
  5. Open the web portal at http://localhost:5180 in a browser and sign in as Floor Warden, BH1 (S-FW) on My approvals, in case the President wants to see the warden's side.
  6. Have the receipt image ready in the phone's gallery. On the simulator, drag receipt5k.jpg (a KL payment receipt for ₹5,000) onto the Simulator window to add it to Photos.

Act 1 · A new student joins Meghana 6 min

  1. Open the app. The KL logo, then the hostel. Students sign in with their university ID. Tap New registration: Meghana Reddy.
  2. Home says she has not applied. Tap Apply for a room: choose Girls Hostel 1, a room type (fee and open beds shown), accept the rules, Apply.
  3. The registration fee is paid on the university payment portal; the receipt is uploaded here. Tap Pay all ₹5,000 → I paid on the university portal → From gallery, pick the receipt, type the reference TXN20261011B1021, Send the receipt.
  4. Pull down to refresh after a few seconds: Receipt matches (the system read the amount, date, reference and her ID), then With the Chief Warden, then Approved. Two levels of approval, same as every request in the hostel. The portal shows the warden's view if asked.
  5. Registration moves to Choose your room: tap an open bed, Take this bed. Then Two instalments, South Indian, Confirm plan.
  6. Pay all ₹1,68,000 → Pay online now. In production this is the university gateway. Refresh: the office verifies the fees, the Chief Warden approves, the key is issued. Home now shows her room.

Act 2 · Daily life Meghana, now a resident 8 min

  1. Outing. Tap Ask for outing, fill reason, place, times, phone, Submit. Refresh: Floor Warden approved, Chief Warden approved, Show pass: the QR gate pass. Security scans this at the gate.
  2. Sick leave. Menu → Sick leave: what is wrong, from, until, Report. Refresh: the Floor Warden visited and chose a light diet; approved; meals now go to her room, the mess has her on its sick list.
  3. Repairs. Repairs → My room → Fan, describe, Raise ticket. Refresh: checked genuine, repair started, repaired. Tap Yes, it is fixed.
  4. Mess. Menu → Mess menu (this week's meals). Choose my meals → Next week → pick a dish. Rate my meals → stars. Complaints → A problem with a meal → send; refresh: the mess replied.
  5. Electricity. Menu → Electricity: units left; ₹200 → Pay: units credited at once.
  6. Wallet. Fees page: the wallet card. Any amount paid beyond the dues sits here and pays the next fee in one tap.

Act 3 · Next year Nabin or Meghana 3 min

  1. Home → Renew my room: keep the bed, move, or leave. Tap Keep → Renew → Confirm my room → fee plan → Pay. Refresh: fees verified, Chief Warden approved. No registration fee or deposits on renewal; the same bed and key carry on.
  2. Menu → Change room: another room type, the fee difference shown before asking.

If something goes wrong

  • A staff step does not arrive: look at the assistant's Terminal; it prints what it did or why it could not. Pull down to refresh on the phone.
  • The server is down: install demo/KL-Hostels-Student-Demo.apk on the Android phone, or run OFFLINE=1 scripts/run-mobile-demo.sh. It is the same app with sample data and needs nothing.
  • To start the whole demo again: scripts/reset-demo.sh, then sign out on the iPhone (Menu → Sign out).
  • The camera does not work in the simulators; use From gallery. On a real phone both work.
