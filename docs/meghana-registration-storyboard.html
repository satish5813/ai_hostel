Meghana joins the hostel

3

She pays the registration fee and sends the receipt

Phone

  • Pays ₹5,000 on the university payment portal as every fee is paid today, and downloads the PDF receipt.
  • In the app: Pay all ₹5,000 → I paid on the university portal → Choose the PDF receipt → types the transaction number, the date → Send the receipt. Photographs are not accepted: only the portal’s PDF.
  • Sees Receipt sent. The Floor Manager is checking it. If the receipt was used before or the amount is wrong, the reason shows in red right there.

System, in seconds

  • Reads the PDF’s own text: amount, date, transaction number, her ID, the KL header.
  • Marks it Receipt matches or Check by eye with what differed, for the warden to see beside the image.
  • Refuses the same file or the same transaction number a second time.

5

She fills in her hostel record, once

Phone · Menu → Parents and profile

  • Add photo: camera or gallery; Security sees it at the gate.
  • My hostel record → Fill in my record: Aadhaar number and a photo of the card, blood group, permanent address with PIN, father's and mother's names and mobiles, local guardian, emergency contact, allergies or regular medicines. Names, numbers and address already known from the ERP are pre-filled.
  • Submit my record. The card turns into a read-only table with a lock: To change anything, ask the Chief Manager.
  • Academic record → Before KL: her 10th and Intermediate marks, with Add or update to add a photo of each certificate.

Staff side

  • Her parents' numbers become family contacts (verified later by OTP; one parent recommends her outings).
  • The Chief Manager sees the full record on Student → Hostel and is the only one who can Edit it. Wardens and the office see it with the Aadhaar number masked.

This scene can happen any time before the key is handed over; the best moment is right after the registration fee, while she waits for the approval.

Staff side

  • The bed shows as held on the Occupancy board. Nobody else can take it.
  • The electricity meter of the room is fitted if it is not there yet (maintenance).

What makes it easy

One phone, one loginFrom applying to the key, Meghana never visits an office or fills a paper form.
Only what applies is shownModules that need a room are black and white until check-in; the status bar always says what comes next.
Receipts, not cash countersShe pays where KL already takes payment and uploads the portal’s PDF receipt; the system reads it and the wardens approve from their inbox.
The phone keeps up by itselfEvery page refreshes every few seconds; each approval arrives as a message. No pull-to-refresh, no phone call to ask "is it done?".
Two levels, every timeFloor Manager checks, Chief Manager approves: receipts, bed, outings, sick leave, room change. One rule, learned once.
Her record, filled onceAadhaar, address, parents, emergency contact go in one form; afterwards only the Chief Manager can change them.
Nothing is lostEvery step has a number, a time and a name: HR-2026-00062, PP-2026-00004, K-0077. The Chief Manager sees the whole trail on one card.
Same on Android and iPhoneThe same app, the same screens; the web portal for staff works on any browser.
