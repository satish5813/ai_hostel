From application to a key in hand, run by four logins

This is the working flow for the first release. It covers only Workflows A to F of the Hostel Data Model v2.1: registration and allotment, mapping, electricity, tracking, outings and sick leave. Everything else (mess, tickets, purchase, convention, academics, discipline, vacating) waits for a later release.

Four logins, nothing else

The sign-in page will offer only these four. The other roles in the document (Floor Warden, Office Staff, Accounts, Security and the rest) remain as staff records that the system maps and notifies, but they do not sign in. Their tasks move to one of the four logins, as listed in Who does what.

Built firstAdministrator

Sets up hostels, floors, rooms, beds, fees, students, staff, meters and rules. Gives the other logins and their hostels. Approves nothing.

SecondChief Warden

Gives the final approval for allotments, outings and sick leave. Handles exceptions, blocking and alerts. Sees every hostel in their scope.

ThirdRoom Allotment

The hostel office. Works the allotment queue, verifies fees (first level), checks students in with the key, the handover list and the meter reading.

FourthStudent

Registers, pays, accepts a room, chooses the fee plan and mess, confirms check-in. Also electricity, outings and sick leave.

Parents and the Dean act through one-time links sent by SMS or email, without an account (decisions D2 and D3, built).

Size of the estate

The application is designed for the full estate, not for a demo. These figures assume 30 to 40 rooms on each floor.

10–15hostels
12–13floors each
30–40rooms a floor
3,600–7,800rooms in all
≈ 11,000–23,000beds, at three a room on average

What that size changes

  • Rooms are generated, not typed. The Administrator builds a hostel in one step: floors G to 12, rooms per floor, which room numbers are which room type. Beds come from the room type. An Excel upload does the same for buildings that don't follow a pattern.
  • One numbering rule everywhere. Floor plus two-digit room number, then the bed letter. For example, BH3-1207-B is hostel BH3, floor 12, room 07, bed B, and ground-floor rooms are G01 to G40. The same code appears on the screens, the QR label and the key tag.
  • Screens work one floor at a time. A floor board has at most 40 rooms and about 160 beds. Hostel and estate totals are counted in the database, so no screen loads 20,000 beds.
  • Search reaches everything. A student ID, name or room code finds the bed in under half a second, across all hostels.
  • The registration rush is planned for. Up to 20,000 students may apply within a few days. Payment confirmations can arrive twice without harm. A bed can be held by only one offer, which the database enforces, so two officers can't give the same bed away. Expired holds are released every few minutes.
  • Lists are paged in the database. Lists show 50 to 200 rows at a time, with filters by hostel, floor, room type, stage and gender.
  • Tested at full size before go-live. A generator creates 15 hostels × 13 floors × 40 rooms with students in every stage. Targets: floor board under 1 second, search under 0.5 seconds, dashboards under 2 seconds.

Rules the system enforces

The database applies these whichever screen is used, so no login can break them. All are from the v2.1 document except the last two, which were added for the two approval levels.

WFRule
AOne registration per student per academic year.
AA room is offered only after the registration fee is paid.
AA bed has only one pending offer or one active stay; a student has only one pending offer.
AThe student's gender must match the hostel's.
AA blocked bed, or a bed in a room under maintenance, is never offered.
AA fee plan exists only for an accepted room. Two instalments means exactly two hostel demands; mess is always one.
ACheck-in needs level 1 fee verification and level 2 Chief Warden approval.
AAadhaar, PAN and bank details are never stored in the hostel database.
BOne active QR label per space. Every floor with occupied beds needs a Floor Warden; floors without one are reported.
COne active meter per room. A reading can't be lower than the last one. Units and excess are calculated, never typed. The meter link opens and closes with check-in and check-out.
DNo movement is stored without current consent. An exit at the campus gate without an approved outing raises an alert. Movements and look-ups can be added, never changed.
ELevels go in order; a rejection ends the request. Only a verified parent contact can recommend. A day or more gets no QR pass until the Dean approves.
FSick leave can't be recommended without the visit details. Only the Chief Warden approves. A missed room meal raises an alert.
AllEvery change records who made it and when, in a log that can't be edited.

Build order

Each stage ends with something you can try in the browser. Stage 0 removes the extra logins before anything else changes.

Stage 0 · Four logins and full-size data

The base everything else is tested on.

  • Sign-in page with four choices only. Menus, home pages and the API accept only those four roles.
  • Logins carry a hostel scope; a Chief Warden or Room Allotment login sees only its hostels.
  • Test-data generator for 15 hostels × 13 floors × 40 rooms, with students at every step.

Stage 1 · Administrator portal

Use cases AD-01 to AD-09.

  • Room builder (new); set-up board reworked as a readiness check; policy screen for the window, hold and due dates.
  • Logins screen for the two staff logins and their hostels.
  • Accepted when a new hostel of 13 floors can be built, fee-set, staffed and marked ready in one sitting.

Stage 2 · Chief Warden portal

Use cases CW-01 to CW-09.

  • Overview across hostels; exceptions inbox (new); outings and sick leave reworked for one decision.
  • Accepted when a Chief Warden can clear a day's approvals for 15 hostels from the overview without searching.

Stage 3 · Room Allotment portal

Use cases RA-01 to RA-07.

  • Allotment desk reworked for paging and batch auto-allot; check-in desk with the student's confirmation; search across hostels.
  • Accepted when two officers working the same hostel can't offer the same bed, and a check-in takes under two minutes.

Stage 4 · Student portal

Use cases ST-01 to ST-06.

  • Home tracker; registration with consent; check-in confirmation (new).
  • Accepted when a student can go from registration to checked in on a phone, with every step visible to the right office login.

Stage 5 · Workflows C to F end to end

Use cases ST-07 to ST-09, CW-05 to CW-08.

  • Electricity recharge, outings with the parent and Dean links, sick leave with the mess list, alerts.

Stage 6 · Full-size trial

Run the whole flow on the generated estate; check the speed targets; fix; hand over for testing by the hostel office.

Decisions, accepted and built

All eight recommended answers were accepted on 3 Oct 2026 and are now in the application (migration 098). Bed letters and room numbers apply to rooms built from now on; existing beds were re-lettered.

built D1 · Who verifies fees?

The document gives this to the Accounts section, which no longer has a login. Recommended: the Room Allotment login verifies (level 1), as it does today.

built D2 · Outing approval without a Floor Warden login

The document has Floor Warden (level 1) then Chief Warden (level 2). Recommended: after the parent's recommendation, the Chief Warden decides once. The Floor Warden is informed by SMS.

built D3 · Outings of a day or more need the Dean

Recommended: the Dean gets a one-time approval link by SMS or email, like the parent, with no login.

built D4 · Sick leave needs an in-person visit

Recommended: the Floor Warden still visits; the Chief Warden records who visited, when, and what they found, then approves.

built D5 · Gate in and out without a Security login

Recommended: gate readers and cameras record movements on their own. A manual entry desk sits inside the Chief Warden portal for when a device is down.

built D6 · Mess sick list without a Mess login

Recommended: the list is sent to the mess in-charge by SMS before each meal and is visible in the Chief Warden portal.

built D7 · How many Chief Wardens?

Recommended: one login type; the Administrator gives each Chief Warden and each Room Allotment login a set of hostels (one, a group, or all).

built D8 · Room numbering

Recommended: floor plus two-digit room (G01, 1207), bed letter after it, hostel code in front: BH3-1207-B.
